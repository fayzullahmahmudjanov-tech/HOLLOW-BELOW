THE HOLLOW BELOW

You woke up in a place that hums. Something tall is walking its halls, and it knows the smell of fear.

Move quietly. Crouch in the dark to disappear. Think before you move: this place punishes the careless.

The door opens onto a mirror.

In it stands the tall figure, wearing a coat that is yours.

It was never hunting you. It was carrying you out of the fire, and you spent years believing it was the thing that burned.
